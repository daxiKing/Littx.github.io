What is LITTX?

LITTX is a Minecraft 1.8.9 client project with free and premium editions.

What is the difference between Monthly and Forever?

Monthly costs $5 and provides 30 days of access. Forever costs $15 and provides permanent LITTX+ membership.

How can I pay?

The planned checkout supports official Paysafecard merchant payments and voucher redemption. Payment methods must be configured and verified on the server before purchases can be completed.

What happens when my Monthly membership expires?

Premium downloads and membership benefits become unavailable until the membership is renewed. Your account and purchase history remain available.

How do vouchers work?

Valid vouchers are checked by the server. If a voucher is accepted, the corresponding membership is activated and recorded in your account.
