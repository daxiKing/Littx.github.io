Need answers?

Frequently
asked.

What is LITTX?

LITTX is a Minecraft 1.8.9 client project with four membership editions.

What is the difference between the editions?

Free provides public content, Plus introduces premium access, Ultimate adds advanced benefits, and Supporter is the highest membership tier.

Can I choose between 30 days and Forever?

Yes. Plus, Ultimate and Supporter each have a 30-day option and a one-time Forever option. Free is always free.

What happens when my 30-day membership expires?

Premium access expires after the membership period. Your account and purchase history should remain available.

Does Forever mean lifetime access?

Forever is intended as a one-time purchase for permanent access to the purchased membership tier, subject to the published membership terms.

How can I pay?

Payment options will be displayed when the official payment provider and secure checkout backend are configured.
